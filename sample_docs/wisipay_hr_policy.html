CHAPTER 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Annual Leave (Earned Leave / PL)

All confirmed employees are entitled to 18 days of Paid Leave (PL) per calendar year, accruing at 1.5 days per month. Leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days across consecutive years. Any balance above 30 days will be encashed at the end of Q1 of the following year at the employee's basic salary rate.

1.2 Sick Leave (SL)

Employees are granted 12 days of Sick Leave per year, non-encashable and non-transferable. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is mandatory for any sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. SL does not carry forward.

1.3 Casual Leave (CL)

6 days of Casual Leave per year for personal/emergency purposes. CL cannot be combined with PL or public holidays, and cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days. CL lapses at year-end.

1.4 Maternity Leave

As per the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017:

  • Primary caregiver (birthing parent): 26 weeks fully paid leave.
  • Applicable for first two children. Third child onward: 12 weeks paid leave.
  • Adoption of a child below 3 months: 12 weeks paid leave for the adopting mother.
  • Commissioning mothers (surrogacy): 12 weeks paid leave.
  • WisiPay additionally provides a Creche Allowance of INR 3,000/month for 6 months post-maternity.

1.5 Paternity Leave

WisiPay offers 10 days of paid Paternity Leave (beyond statutory requirements) to be availed within 90 days of childbirth or adoption. Paternity leave is available for all legal guardians regardless of gender identity.

1.6 Bereavement Leave

5 days for first-degree relatives (spouse, parents, children, siblings). 2 days for second-degree relatives (grandparents, in-laws). Additional unpaid leave may be considered at the manager's discretion.

1.7 Public Holidays

WisiPay follows a National Holiday List of 10 days plus 3 Restricted Holidays (RH) chosen by the employee from the state holiday calendar. The list is published by the HR team every December for the following year.

2.2 Performance Bonus

Variable pay is disbursed annually in June, linked to:

  • Company performance (50% weight): Revenue target achievement vs actual.
  • Team/Department KPIs (30% weight): Reviewed quarterly.
  • Individual performance (20% weight): Based on OKR score (0–5 scale).

Minimum OKR score of 3.0 is required for bonus eligibility. Employees who resign before 31 March of the bonus year are not eligible.

2.3 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

WisiPay operates an ESOP pool of 8% of fully diluted equity. Grants are made annually to Senior Engineer level and above. Standard vesting: 1-year cliff, 4-year linear vesting (25% per year). Exercise price is set at fair market value at the date of grant. ESOPs lapse 90 days after resignation.

2.4 Health Insurance

  • Group Mediclaim: INR 5 lakh per employee (self + spouse + 2 children).
  • Top-up cover: Employee can purchase additional INR 10 lakh at group rates.
  • Personal Accident Cover: INR 25 lakh for all employees.
  • Term Life Insurance: 5x Annual CTC (minimum INR 50 lakh).
  • OPD Reimbursement: INR 15,000 per year (dental and vision included).

2.5 Other Benefits

  • Internet Reimbursement: INR 1,200/month (work-from-home employees).
  • Phone Allowance: INR 800/month (for roles requiring client communication).
  • Learning & Development Budget: INR 25,000/year per employee for courses, certifications.
  • Gym/Wellness Reimbursement: INR 8,000/year.
  • Meal Vouchers: INR 2,200/month (Sodexo or equivalent).

CHAPTER 3: CODE OF CONDUCT

3.1 Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any financial interest (direct or indirect) in a WisiPay competitor, merchant, or bank partner. Holdings above 1% equity in any such entity must be disclosed within 15 days of joining or upon acquisition. Failure to disclose is grounds for immediate termination.

3.2 Insider Trading Policy

As WisiPay is preparing for an IPO (targeted FY2026), all employees are classified as designated persons under SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations. Trading windows are closed 48 hours before and 24 hours after any material announcement. Pre-clearance is required for trades above INR 5 lakh.

3.3 Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption (ABAC)

WisiPay has a zero-tolerance policy for bribery. Gifts from vendors or merchants must not exceed INR 1,000 in value and must be declared to the manager. Facilitating payments to government officials for routine services is strictly prohibited. Violations attract termination and may be referred to law enforcement agencies.

3.4 Data Privacy & Confidentiality

All employees must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) on Day 1. Payment data, merchant credentials, and customer PII must never be shared via personal email, WhatsApp, or unencrypted channels. Violation of PCI-DSS data handling policies may result in personal liability under the IT Act, 2000.

3.5 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

WisiPay is fully compliant with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. An Internal Committee (IC) is constituted at every office location. Complaints must be submitted within 3 months of the incident. Anonymous reporting is available via the WisiPay Ethics Hotline: 1800-XXX-XXXX.

3.6 Disciplinary Procedure

Disciplinary actions follow a progressive model:

  1. Verbal Warning (documented in HRIS)
  2. Written Warning
  3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP — 60 days)
  4. Termination (with cause)

Gross misconduct (fraud, data theft, violence) may result in immediate termination at step 4 without prior warnings.

CHAPTER 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY

4.1 Work-from-Home Eligibility

Employees at Associate Engineer and above with a minimum of 6 months' tenure are eligible for a hybrid schedule of 3 days office / 2 days WFH. Engineering, Product and Design roles may opt for fully remote with manager approval. Support, Sales and Operations roles are primarily office-based.

4.2 Equipment Policy

  • WisiPay issues a company laptop to all employees. Personal devices are not permitted for accessing internal systems.
  • If a laptop is lost or damaged due to negligence, the employee bears 50% of the replacement cost (capped at INR 20,000).
  • Monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic chairs: One-time reimbursement of INR 12,000 for WFH setup (submitted within 90 days of joining).
