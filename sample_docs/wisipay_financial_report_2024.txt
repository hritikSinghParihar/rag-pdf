EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
WisiPay Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. recorded its strongest fiscal year in company history
during FY2024. Total revenue crossed INR 480 crore, representing a 67% year-over-year growth.
Net profit after tax (PAT) stood at INR 54.2 crore, with an EBITDA margin of 18.4%.
The merchant base expanded from 12,400 to 31,700 active merchants across 22 Indian states.

SECTION 4: PRODUCT UPDATES — FY2024
================================================================

4.1 Payment Gateway
--------------------
  - Launched Smart Routing Engine v3.0 with ML-based least-cost routing.
  - Added support for 14 new payment methods including CBDC pilot wallets.
  - Reduced API response latency from 420ms to 180ms (p95).
  - PCI-DSS Level 1 certification renewed in September 2024.

4.2 Payouts Platform
---------------------
  - Real-time IMPS/UPI payout throughput increased to 2,400 TPS.
  - Bulk payout file processing now supports CSV, XLSX and API batch modes.
  - Introduced atomic TPS-limit enforcement using Redis Lua scripts to prevent
    double-spend under high concurrency.
  - Launched vendor payment portal with CFO dashboard and approval workflows.

4.3 Recurring Payments
------------------------
  - NACH mandate success rate improved to 93.7% through intelligent retry logic.
  - UPI AutoPay (UPI 2.0) mandates onboarded 1.2M end-users by March 2025.
  - Introduced smart debit scheduling to avoid low-balance failures.

4.4 Compliance & Risk
----------------------
  - RBI Payment Aggregator (PA) license application submitted — status: pending.
  - Implemented AML transaction monitoring with real-time rule engine.
  - Launched biometric merchant KYC (DigiLocker + Aadhaar OTP) reducing
    onboarding time from 72 hours to 4 hours.
  - Fraud loss rate maintained at 0.003% of TPV.

================================================================
SECTION 5: RISK FACTORS
================================================================

  5.1 Regulatory Risk
  --------------------
  The RBI's Payment Aggregator guidelines require WisiPay to obtain PA licensing
  by December 2025. Non-compliance would restrict onboarding of new merchants.
  We have engaged external legal counsel and submitted all required documents.

  5.2 Credit Risk
  ----------------
  INR 22.4 crore of settlement funds are held in nodal accounts. Returns and
  chargebacks are backstopped by merchant deposits averaging 3 days' float.

  5.3 Cybersecurity Risk
  -----------------------
  Two attempted DDoS events were mitigated in Q3 with zero service downtime.
  We maintain a bug bounty programme and conduct quarterly penetration tests.

  5.4 Concentration Risk
  -----------------------
  Top 10 merchants contribute 18.3% of TPV. Loss of any single top merchant
  would reduce quarterly revenue by an estimated INR 8–12 crore.
